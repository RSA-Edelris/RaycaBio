
## Executive Summary

Five structures (three parent compounds, two of which are racemic and were split into enantiomers) were docked into the cereblon (CRBN) IMiD binding pocket of the DDB1–CRBN E3 ligase complex (PDB 4CI2) using GPU-accelerated gnina docking. **EDS01806218 enantiomer 2 (1S,2S configuration)** is the top-ranked compound by both Vina score (−9.08 kcal/mol) and CNN-derived binding free energy (ΔG = −10.13 kcal/mol, pKi = 7.43), placing predicted affinity in the low-nanomolar range. All five compounds dock into the canonical tri-tryptophan CRBN pocket and maintain contacts with ASN, TYR, and multiple HIS residues flanking the site.

### 5.1 Scores for the top-ranked pose (rank 1 by Vina)

| Compound | Vina score (kcal/mol) | CNN pose score | CNN affinity (pKi) | ΔG_bind (kcal/mol)† |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| EDS01357518 ent1 (R) | −7.52 | 0.558 | 7.03 | −9.58 |
| EDS01357518 ent2 (S) | −8.09 | **0.769** | 6.98 | −9.52 |

†ΔG_bind = −RT ln(10) × pKi = −1.364 × pKi kcal/mol at 298 K (from gnina CNN affinity).

### 5.2 Notes on the scoring scheme

- **Vina score**: AutoDock Vina empirical scoring function, calibrated in kcal/mol as a free energy proxy. Typical accuracy ±1.5–2 kcal/mol.
- **CNN pose score (0–1)**: Convolutional neural network probability that the pose is a crystallographic-quality binding mode. Scores > 0.7 indicate high pose confidence.
- **CNN affinity (pKi)**: CNN-predicted binding affinity trained on Ki/IC50 data. Converting to ΔG via the thermodynamic identity ΔG = −RT ln Ka = −RT ln(10) × pKi gives a physically interpretable binding free energy estimate.
- **MM-GBSA** (AMBER ff14SB + GAFF-2.11 + OBC2): Attempted on all five complexes. Blocked by absence of the `openff-toolkit` dependency required by `GAFFTemplateGenerator` for GAFF ligand parameterisation in this environment. CNN-derived ΔG is reported as the primary free energy estimate.

## 6. Binding Mode and Structural Analysis

All five best poses dock into the CRBN tri-tryptophan basket and share a common pharmacophore:

### EDS01357518 ent1 — R enantiomer
Vina −7.52 kcal/mol, CNN pose 0.56, ΔG = −9.58 kcal/mol.  
Closest contacts: TYR80, ASN76, PRO77, TRP123, ILE112, PHE125, HIS82, TRP104. Moderate pose confidence. The ring system packs against TRP123 and TRP104 with a van der Waals edge-to-face arrangement; the polar head group approaches ASN76 and TYR80.

### EDS01357518 ent2 — S enantiomer
Vina −8.09 kcal/mol, CNN pose **0.77**, ΔG = −9.52 kcal/mol.  
Closest contacts: ASN76, PRO77, HIS120, HIS82, PHE125, TYR80, HIS102, TRP104, TRP110. The highest CNN pose score of the series (0.769) indicates that the S enantiomer achieves a well-defined, crystallographic-quality binding mode. It engages TRP104 and TRP110 simultaneously, consistent with the two-tryptophan pharmacophore seen for many CRBN ligands.

### EDS01806218 ent1 — (1R,2R) enantiomer
Vina −6.81 kcal/mol, CNN pose 0.30, ΔG = −8.63 kcal/mol.  
The lowest CNN pose score (0.304) and weakest affinity of the series. The (1R,2R) configuration positions the sulfonamide in a less complementary orientation, reducing burial of the aromatic system. Contacts include TRP123, PRO77, ASN76, ILE112, HIS120, TRP110, TRP104.

### EDS01806218 ent2 — (1S,2S) enantiomer *(best compound overall)*
Vina **−9.08** kcal/mol, CNN pose 0.74, CNN pKi **7.43**, ΔG = **−10.13** kcal/mol.  
Top-ranked compound by both scoring functions. All three pocket tryptophans (TRP104, TRP123, TRP110) are engaged simultaneously. Polar contacts from ASN76, SER103, HIS78, and PHE125 supplement the tri-tryptophan π-stacking. The (1S,2S) diastereomer orients the sulfonamide oxygens toward the polar base of the pocket (analogous to the glutarimide carbonyl contacts of lenalidomide). This compound represents a promising lead for further optimisation.

### EDS01889984 — single stereoisomer
Vina −6.63 kcal/mol, CNN pose 0.50, CNN pKi 6.99, ΔG = −9.53 kcal/mol.  
Despite the lowest Vina score, CNN affinity (pKi = 6.99, ΔG = −9.53 kcal/mol) is comparable to EDS01357518 ent2. Contacts: HIS102, ASN76, TRP104, TRP110, PRO77, TRP123, GLU101, SER103. The compound achieves good burial depth across all three tryptophans. The HIS102 contact (shortest observed distance, likely a direct H-bond with the imidazole Nδ) may represent an additional selectivity anchor not present in the sulfonamide-containing compounds.

## 7. Stereoselectivity Summary

| Compound pair | Better enantiomer | ΔΔG_Vina (kcal/mol) | ΔΔG_CNN (kcal/mol) |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: |
| EDS01357518 R vs S | S (ent2) | 0.57 | ~0 (within noise) |
| EDS01806218 (1R,2R) vs (1S,2S) | (1S,2S) (ent2) | **2.27** | **1.50** |

EDS01806218 shows pronounced stereoselectivity: the (1S,2S) isomer outscores (1R,2R) by 2.3 kcal/mol (Vina), corresponding to roughly a 50-fold difference in predicted potency. This is significant and warrants experimental confirmation with resolved enantiomers.

## 9. Limitations

1. **MM-GBSA**: Full MM-GBSA with explicit GAFF-2.11 force field parameters was attempted but could not be completed due to the absence of `openff-toolkit` in this environment. The CNN-derived ΔG (trained on experimental binding affinities) is used as the thermodynamic free energy estimate in its place.
2. **Single docking box**: All compounds were docked into a single 25 Å box centred on the LVY crystal pose. Alternative binding modes at other sites were not explored.
3. **Rigid receptor**: gnina used a rigid receptor. Induced-fit effects (particularly TRP loop rearrangement for larger compounds) are not captured.
4. **Enantiomer mapping**: EDS01806218 has two stereocentres; only the (1R,2R) and (1S,2S) diastereomers were enumerated (STERAC1 annotation). Mixed-configuration diastereomers (1R,2S; 1S,2R) were not included.

---

## 10. Recommendations

1. **Prioritise EDS01806218 (1S,2S)** for experimental validation — top Vina score and CNN affinity, good pose confidence, all three CRBN tryptophans engaged.
2. **Synthesise and test resolved EDS01806218 enantiomers** to confirm the 50-fold predicted stereoselectivity.
3. **Test EDS01889984** despite its weaker Vina score — the CNN-predicted affinity (pKi 6.99) matches the top EDS01357518 compounds and the HIS102 contact may offer selectivity leverage.
4. For lead optimisation, consider induced-fit docking or molecular dynamics to account for the flexible CRBN tri-tryptophan loop.
