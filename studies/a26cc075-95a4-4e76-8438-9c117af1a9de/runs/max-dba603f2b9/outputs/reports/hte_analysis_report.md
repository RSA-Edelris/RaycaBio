
## Study overview

A 96-well high-throughput experimentation (HTE) screen was analysed for a copper-catalysed reaction. The design covers **12 ligands × 2 bases × 2 Cu sources × 2 solvents = 96 conditions**. HPLC-UV data were collected for starting material (SM, MW 194.97), internal standard (IS, MW 273.38, RT ≈ 5.36 min), and desired product (DP, MW 379.42, RT ≈ 4.28 min).

## Methods

### Normalisation
Product response was normalised to the internal standard:

```
norm_yield = Area(DP) / Area(IS)
```

Where `Area(DP) = 0` (peak absent), `norm_yield = 0`. No response-factor correction was applied (same chromophore class assumed); values are relative area ratios, not absolute yields.

### Plate mapping
Wells 001–096 were mapped row-major to the 96-well plate grid (A1–A12 = wells 1–12, B1–B12 = wells 13–24, … H1–H12 = wells 85–96).

### Condition breakdown
Three condition-level pivot heatmaps were generated by averaging `norm_yield` over the two non-focal variables:
- **Ligand × Solvent** (averaged over base and Cu source)
- **Ligand × Base** (averaged over solvent and Cu source)
- **Ligand × Cu Source** (averaged over base and solvent)

## Interpretation

**Ligand choice is the dominant variable.** DMCyDA occupies the entire top 7 hits; all other ligands are at least 3× lower. Among the non-DMCyDA ligands, Oxine with CuI in DMF shows modest activity (~0.28–0.29), which may warrant a follow-up.

Within DMCyDA conditions: **Dioxane > DMF** and **K₂CO₃ > K₃PO₄**. Cu source shows a smaller effect; both CuI and Cu(OTf)₂ are viable.

## Recommendation

**Primary condition:** DMCyDA / K₂CO₃ / Cu(OTf)₂ / Dioxane (well 068, norm. yield 1.689)

**Backup condition:** DMCyDA / K₂CO₃ / CuI / Dioxane (well 067, norm. yield 1.021) — lower reagent cost, same base/solvent profile.

A dose–response or scale-up experiment with DMCyDA under K₂CO₃/Dioxane conditions, varying Cu source and stoichiometry, is the logical next step.

## Limitations

- No response-factor correction between DP and IS; reported values are area ratios, not molar yields.
- No plate quality metrics (Z′, SSMD) computable: no dedicated positive/negative control wells were present in the plate map.
- SM peak was absent in most wells; this prevents independent conversion tracking from the SM channel.
