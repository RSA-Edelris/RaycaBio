## Design unchanged from dark version

**Fixed:** DMCyDA ligand throughout all 96 wells

| Axis | Levels |
|------|--------|
| Rows A–H | Base × Solvent: K₂CO₃, Cs₂CO₃, K₃PO₄ in Dioxane/DMF + K₂CO₃ in DMSO/MeCN |
| Cols 1–12 | Cu source × loading: CuI, Cu(OTf)₂, CuBr, CuCl, Cu(OAc)₂, Cu₂O at 5 and 10 mol% |

Color encoding:
- Blue (CuI), light blue (CuBr), lighter blue (CuCl) — Cu(I) halides
- Amber (Cu(OTf)₂), orange (Cu(OAc)₂) — Cu triflate / acetate
- Violet (Cu₂O) — Cu(I) oxide
- Dashed gold rings — direct screen 1 control wells (row A, cols 1–4)
- Brighter fill = 5 mol%; dimmed fill = 10 mol%
