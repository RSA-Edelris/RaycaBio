Mablink ADC — Linear Synthetic Scheme

Convergent 3-stage synthesis of a cathepsin B-cleavable ADC linker-payload for thiol-maleimide antibody conjugation.
Payload: Exatecan (DXd-type Topo-I inhibitor) · Cleavage: Ac-Val-Ala · Spacer: branched PAB carbamate · Hydrophilic arm: Polysar-10 (polysarcosine 10-mer) · Warhead: maleimide (DAR 4).

Verification

Provenance: Mablink SMILES from researcher-supplied Projets Custom.sdf (compound 1, MW 1962). All intermediate SMILES validated with RDKit. Building blocks commercially sourced or synthesised by standard literature protocols.
