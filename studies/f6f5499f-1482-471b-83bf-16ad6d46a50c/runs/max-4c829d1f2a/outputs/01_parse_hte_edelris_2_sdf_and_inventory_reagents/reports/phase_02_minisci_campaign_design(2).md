# Phase 2 — Design Photocatalytic Minisci HTE Campaign + Plate Map

## Reaction identity
- **Type:** Photocatalytic NHP-ester Minisci radical C–H alkylation (no Ni required)
- **Electrophile/acceptor:** Brc1cncc2ccccc12 — bromoquinoline (radical acceptor at C2)
- **NHP ester / radical precursor:** CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N1CCC(CC1)C(=O)OC2C(=O)c3ccccc3C2=O
  (N-Boc piperidine-4-yl radical generated by reductive fragmentation + decarboxylation)
- **Product:** CC(C)(C)OC(=O)N1CCC(CC1)c2ncc(Br)c3ccccc23

## Mechanism
1. PC* + NHP-ester → PC⁺ + R• + CO₂ + phthalimide⁻  (oxidative quenching; PC* acts as reductant)
2. R• + quinolinium (protonated) → radical adduct at C2
3. Adduct radical + PC⁺ → quinolinium cation + PC (re-oxidation)
4. Deprotonation → aromatic product

**Ni is NOT required.** The Pd block (20 entries) and Ni block (4 entries) are irrelevant.

## Absolutely necessary missing reagent
**TFA (trifluoroacetic acid, ≥99%) — MUST PURCHASE**
No Brønsted acid is present in any of the 74 kit entries.
Use 1.0 equiv vs quinoline. Without protonation, radical addition rate and regioselectivity
to quinoline C2 drop substantially (literature precedent: >10× rate difference ± acid).

## Design — full 4 × 3 × 2 factorial = 24 wells

**Why this design suits the factor set:**
All three factors are categorical. The product 4 × 3 × 2 = 24 exactly matches one plate,
making a full factorial achievable. Every main effect and 2-way interaction is estimable
with no sacrifice; no fractional design is needed.

**Plate layout:**
- Row A–D → Photocatalyst
- Column pairs 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6 → Solvent (MeCN / DMSO / Dioxane)
- Odd columns (1, 3, 5) → TFA 1 equiv; even columns (2, 4, 6) → no acid

**Pre-registered model:**
Y_ijk = µ + α_i(PC) + β_j(Solv) + γ_k(Acid) + (αβ)_ij + (αγ)_ik + (βγ)_jk + ε_ijk

| Source | df |
|--------|----|
| PC | 3 |
| Solvent | 2 |
| Acid | 1 |
| PC × Solvent | 6 |
| PC × Acid | 3 |
| Solvent × Acid | 2 |
| **PC × Solv × Acid (error)** | **6** |
| **Total** | **23** |

3-way interaction (6 df) used as error surrogate (assumed negligible at screening stage).
Transform: arcsin√(p/100) if conversions cluster near 0 or 100%.

| Round | Plate | Variables | Decision rule |

**Bayesian BO vs. full factorial:**
The 24-condition search space equals the plate — BO offers no advantage in Round 1.
BO is beneficial from Round 2 onward when continuous variables expand the search space
beyond plate capacity (e.g. 5 acid equiv × 3 NHP equiv × 4 PC loadings × 3 temperatures = 180 points).

  PC × Solvent combinations simultaneously; it is not confounded with any other factor ✓
