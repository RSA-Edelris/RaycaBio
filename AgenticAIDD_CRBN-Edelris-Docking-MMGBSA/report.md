## Key Findings

### Top hits (GBSA ΔG < −43 kcal/mol)

**EDEL-CRBN-0009_ent** (−44.75 kcal/mol) ranks #1 by MM-GBSA despite not being the top Vina scorer. **EDEL-CRBN-0013** and **EDEL-CRBN-0009** (the enantiomeric pair) follow at −43.80 and −43.75 kcal/mol — the pair are near-equivalent by GBSA (Δ = 0.05 kcal/mol), consistent with a symmetric binding mode.

**EDEL-CRBN-0005** and **EDEL-CRBN-0005_ent** (ranks 8 and 5) are both among the top 8 by GBSA and both have the highest Vina scores (−10.17, −10.21 kcal/mol), making this scaffold the most consistently top-ranked across all scoring methods.

### Duplicate entries

EDEL-CRBN-0011 and EDEL-CRBN-0012 (and their _ent variants) have identical MM-GBSA energies and component values, indicating these are the same structure in the input SDF.

### Outliers

**EDEL-CRBN-0010** (rank 32, −21.33 kcal/mol) and **EDEL-CRBN-0007** (rank 29, −29.43 kcal/mol) are the weakest binders. Both show unusually weak VDW contributions (−27.7 and −34.4 kcal/mol vs. ~−40 to −48 for top hits), suggesting poor shape complementarity in the pocket.

**EDEL-CRBN-0006_ent** (rank 31) has an anomalously large EGB penalty (+15.90 kcal/mol), the highest in the series, driving its poor ranking despite moderate VDW.

## Caveats

- Gasteiger charges are less accurate than AM1-BCC for polar groups; systematic error expected to largely cancel within this congeneric series.
- 20 ps MD provides local relaxation only; not converged for absolute binding free energies.
- EDEL-CRBN-0001 std err = 1.24 kcal/mol (larger than others at ~0.3–0.5) because it was run on a 2-frame test trajectory; the 50-frame value would be more precise.
- 0011 = 0012 duplication should be investigated at the input SDF level.
